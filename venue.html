Travel information

By train. Magdeburg Hauptbahnhof (main station) has direct connections to Berlin (about 1.5–2 hours), Hannover (about 1.5 hours) and Leipzig (about 1 hour). Timetables and tickets are on bahn.de.

By plane. The nearest airports are Leipzig/Halle (LEJ), Berlin Brandenburg (BER) and Hannover (HAJ). All three have train connections to Magdeburg, taking roughly 1.5–2.5 hours.

From the station to the campus. Take tram line 1 from the stop in front of the main station to the stop Universität (about 10 minutes), or walk about 20 minutes. Local timetables are on mvbnet.de.

By car. From the A2 motorway, take the exit Magdeburg-Zentrum and follow the B1 towards the city centre to the large roundabout at Universitätsplatz.

Food options

Mensa UniCampus. The university canteen, run by the Studentenwerk Magdeburg, is on the campus a few minutes' walk from the lecture room. It serves hot lunches on weekdays, with several dishes to choose from every day, including vegetarian and vegan options. Participants from outside Magdeburg can pay by card; guest prices are a little higher than student prices. The daily menu is on the Studentenwerk Magdeburg website.

Nearby. There are cafés on the campus itself, and the Breiter Weg, Magdeburg's main street, starts right next to Universitätsplatz. Along it and in the city centre you will find bakeries, cafés with lunch menus and restaurants with German and international food, all within a short walk.
